CATBOT

a clockwork repair cat · a wrecked shuttle

A shuttle has crashed on a dead world, and something small survived it: a brass-and-copper clockwork cat that has no idea it is a repair unit. Wind it up, wander the wreck, and find out what is broken.

Ship parts look like toys to a cat, so the ship gets put back together one plaything at a time.

Walking is the only verb. ◀ ▶ or A D to walk (▲ ▼ or W S too, on the deck plan). On a phone, hold the on-screen arrows. Everything else is somewhere to stand. You can also start by walking instead of pressing PLAY.
