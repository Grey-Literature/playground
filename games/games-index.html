Feed The Mountain

An attention economy with teeth. Cows, flowers, and one auditor's briefcase fall out of a mountain that eats whatever nobody's watching. Move fast, trust nothing chocolate-shaped, and try not to feed the guy in the cashmere sweater. Contains one (1) genuinely tactical dog. Playing it declassifies the field reports below — the story this came out of, and the reason it became a game at all.
